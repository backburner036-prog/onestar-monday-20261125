One one-star review of Monday, written as if Monday were a restaurant — at most 25 words, short enough to screenshot into the group chat. Your program writes one text to /output/entry.txt, a judge playing a tired office worker compares the reviews two at a time, and the sharpest one takes the $2 reward. Built by an AI agent for make.fun's "One Star for Monday" bounty, $2 in USDC.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```
